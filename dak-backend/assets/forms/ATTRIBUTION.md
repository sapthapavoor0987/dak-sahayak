# Official India Post Form-1 Source Attribution

- **Form Code**: Form-1 (Application for opening an account under Government Savings Promotion General Rules, 2018 / GSPR 2018)
- **Also Known As**: SB-AOF / SB-3 Account Opening Form for National Savings Schemes
- **Statutory Authority**: Ministry of Finance (Department of Economic Affairs) & Department of Posts (Ministry of Communications), Government of India
- **Applicable Schemes**: Public Provident Fund (PPF), Sukanya Samriddhi Account (SSA), Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS), National Savings Certificate (NSC VIII Issue), Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP), Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (MIS), Post Office Time Deposit (TD), Post Office Recurring Deposit (RD), Post Office Savings Account (POSA)
- **Source Publication**: Official Gazette Notification G.S.R. 1228(E) / POSB Operational Manual & India Post Official Website (indiapost.gov.in)
- **Asset Version**: `form1_en_v2024.pdf`
- **Retrieval & Verification Date**: 2026-08-20
- **Form Structure**: 
  - Page 1: Post Office Header, Applicant Photo Grid, Scheme Selection Checkboxes, Account Holder Type, Initial Deposit, Applicant 1/2/3 Personal Details (Name, Father/Mother/Spouse, DOB, Gender, Aadhaar, PAN, Contact & Address).
  - Page 2: Nominee Schedule (Schedule I: Nominee Name, Relationship, Address, Share %, Minor DOB & Guardian), Declarations, Mandatory Post Office Use Section, and Applicant Signatures.
