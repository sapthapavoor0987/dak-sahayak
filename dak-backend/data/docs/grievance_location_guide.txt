INDIA POST BRANCH LOCATOR, PIN CODE, TIMINGS & GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL GUIDE

1. Branch Locator & PIN Code Services:
- How to Find PIN Code: Search by locality name, city, or district on www.indiapost.gov.in -> Find Pincode tool, or call toll-free helpline 1800 266 6868.
- Nearest POPSK / Aadhaar Centers: Designated Head Post Offices (HPO) provide Passport Seva (POPSK) and Aadhaar enrolment/updates. Located via India Post portal under 'Locate Post Office'.
- Working Hours & Counter Timings:
  * Standard Post Offices: Monday to Saturday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Public transactions typically 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM / 4:00 PM). Closed on Sundays and National Holidays.
  * Night Post Offices: Selected Head Post Offices operate extended counter hours up to 8:30 PM (and on Sundays/Holidays from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM) for Speed Post, booking, and sale of stamps.

2. Grievance Redressal & Complaint Handling:
- How to File Online Complaint:
  * Official India Post Portal: Visit www.indiapost.gov.in -> Register Complaint.
  * CPGRAMS Portal: Register on Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System (pgportal.gov.in).
  * Toll-Free Helpline: Call 1800 266 6868 (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, except Sundays/Holidays).
  * In-Person: Submit written complaint in prescribed form at any Post Office counter.
- Resolution Timelines (Citizen Charter Standards):
  * Speed Post & Parcel Delivery Complaints: Resolved within 7 working days.
  * Registered Post / Ordinary Mail Inquiries: Resolved within 30 working days.
  * Savings Bank & Financial Services Complaints: Resolved within 15 to 30 working days.
  * Postal Life Insurance (PLI / RPLI) Claims & Inquiries: Resolved within 15 working days.
