INDIA POST PAYMENTS BANK (IPPB) & AADHAAR SERVICES OFFICIAL GUIDE

1. IPPB Digital Zero-Balance Savings Account:
- Opening Account: Can be opened instantly via paperless digital onboarding using Aadhaar and PAN through the IPPB Mobile Banking App or at post offices / via Postman using micro-ATM.
- Account Features: Zero balance account, no minimum monthly balance required. Offers 2.0% p.a. interest (paid quarterly). Free Virtual Debit Card for online transactions.
- Limits: Maximum end-of-day balance limit is Rs. 2 Lakhs as per RBI guidelines.

2. Doorstep Banking (DSB) Services:
- How it works: Customers can request banking services at their doorstep via the Postinfo app, IPPB website, or by calling 155299 / 1800 180 7980. Postmen or Gramin Dak Sevaks (GDS) visit the customer's house equipped with biometric micro-ATMs.
- Services Offered: Cash withdrawal/deposit, Aadhaar mobile number update, Digital Life Certificate (DLC) generation, bill payments, money transfers, and IPPB account opening.
- Service Charges: Rs. 20 + GST per successful doorstep visit for financial services.

3. Aadhaar Enrolment & Update Services at Post Offices:
- Services Available: Fresh Aadhaar enrolment (Free of cost), Mobile Number update in Aadhaar (Rs. 50), Address update/Demographic updates (Rs. 50), Child mandatory biometric update for children aged 5 and 15 years (Free of cost), and Child Aadhaar enrolment (CELC - Child Enrolment Lite Client) for 0-5 years at doorstep.
- Passport Seva (POPSK): Post Office Passport Seva Kendras (POPSK) operate in designated Head Post Offices across India to process fresh passport applications and renewals in coordination with MEA.

4. AePS (Aadhaar Enabled Payment System):
- Cash Withdrawal via AePS: Customers of ANY bank linked with Aadhaar can withdraw cash, check balance, or get mini-statements at any Post Office counter or from a Postman/GDS using Aadhaar biometric authentication.
- Limits & Charges: AePS service at India Post counters/doorstep is completely free of charge for customers. Bank-specific daily transaction limits apply (usually up to Rs. 10,000 per transaction).
